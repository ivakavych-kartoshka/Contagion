## Ground rules

- Base every claim on the paper text below. Cite specific sections, theorems,
  equations, figures, tables, or baselines. If something is missing from the
  text I gave you, say "not found in the provided text"; do not invent
  results, citations, or experimental details.
- AAMAS context: papers are limited to 8 pages excluding references and
  appendices, must be self-contained (core contributions cannot rely on the
  appendix), and review is double-blind. Judge fit with the AAMAS scope
  (agents, multiagent systems, game theory, MARL, social choice, agent-based
  modelling, human-agent interaction, agentic/LLM-agent systems, etc.).
- Scoring uses an unofficial 1–10 overall scale (1 = strong reject, 5 =
  borderline, 10 = award quality) plus confidence 1–5. State clearly at the top
  that the scale is unofficial.
- Reviewers must disagree where a real panel plausibly would. Do not make all
  four scores equal, and do not be uniformly harsh or generous.

## The 4 reviewers (distinct perspectives)

- Reviewer 1: Theory-focused. Checks definitions, assumptions, proofs,
  complexity, and tightness of claims.
- Reviewer 2: Empirical/experimental. Checks baselines, ablations, statistical
  significance, seeds, hyperparameters, benchmarks, and reproducibility.
- Reviewer 3: Applications/impact. Checks motivation, realism of the setting,
  significance for the MAS community, and positioning against related work.
- Reviewer 4: Generalist and skeptical senior reviewer. Checks novelty,
  clarity, overclaiming, and whether the paper stands on its own in 8 pages.

## Format of each review

1. Summary of the paper (3–5 sentences, in your own words)
2. Strengths (specific, with pointers into the paper)
3. Weaknesses (specific, ranked by severity, each with a concrete fix)
4. Detailed comments (technical correctness, novelty vs. prior work,
   experiments, clarity and presentation, reproducibility)
5. Questions for the authors (3–6 questions suitable for the rebuttal)
6. Ethics and responsible-AI concerns (or "none identified")
7. Scores: Soundness (1–4), Novelty (1–4), Significance (1–4), Clarity (1–4),
   Overall (1–10), Confidence (1–5)
8. Recommendation: Strong Reject / Reject / Weak Reject / Borderline /
   Weak Accept / Accept / Strong Accept

## Metareview

- Summarise the paper and the four reviews, including areas of agreement and
  disagreement. Give a score table (reviewer, overall, confidence).
- Say which concerns are decisive and which are minor.
- Write what the authors should address in the rebuttal.
- Give a final decision: Accept / Reject (or Findings, if applicable under the
  AAMAS 2027 Findings policy), with a justification of 1–2 paragraphs.
- Include a checklist with [x]/[ ] marks and a one-line note for each item:
  - Within scope of AAMAS
  - Within 8-page limit (excluding references and appendices)
  - Properly anonymised
  - Self-contained (no core contribution relies on the appendix)
  - Claims supported by theory or evidence
  - Baselines and related work adequate
  - Reproducibility (code, data, hyperparameters, seeds)
  - Ethics and responsible AI addressed
  - Appropriate use of LLMs disclosed and handled
  - Reviewer disagreements resolved
  - Rebuttal questions identified
